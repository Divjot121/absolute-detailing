# WINDOW TINTING

Tinting is priced separately from detailing and can be booked on its own or alongside a detail.

## Ceramic Tint

| | |
|---|---|
| Sedan | **$99** |
| SUV | **$110** |

Ceramic film uses non-metallic ceramic particles to block heat and UV.

- Blocks up to 99% of UV rays, protecting seats, dash and trim from fading and cracking
- Rejects a significant portion of infrared heat, so the cabin stays cooler and the AC works less
- **No metal content** — no interference with phone signal, GPS, radio or keyless entry
- Reduces glare from sun and oncoming headlights
- Won't turn purple or bubble the way cheaper dyed films do
- Adds shatter resistance to the glass

---

## Nano Ceramic Tint

| | |
|---|---|
| Sedan | **$150** |
| SUV | **$179** |

Our premium tier. Nano-ceramic particles are finer and more densely packed than standard ceramic film.

- **Highest heat rejection we offer** — noticeably cooler cabin in peak summer
- Superior infrared rejection even at lighter shades, so you can keep visibility and still block heat
- Blocks up to 99% of UV rays
- Sharper optical clarity — less haze looking through the glass, better night visibility
- Completely metal-free — no signal interference
- Longest-lasting colour and heat performance of the two options

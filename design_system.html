Domine 600 — Subheads

Mobile or shop, Brampton to Vaughan.

Public Sans 400 — Body

Public Sans is the same family used on official U.S. government sites — it reads as plain, honest, and easy to trust at a glance, which is the whole job of a "traditional corporate" service brand. Domine carries the traditional, established feel in headlines only; everything else stays sans-serif for readability on small posters and phone screens.

Both are free Google Fonts — zero licensing cost. Load once, use everywhere (posters exported from Canva/Illustrator, and the live website).

4. The CTA button — one rule

Red is reserved only for the action you want taken. It never appears as decoration, a headline colour, or a background. That's what makes it findable in under a second.

Message us on Instagram
Book online

Primary (red, filled) = your highest-intent channel right now — Instagram DM. Secondary (navy outline) = website booking, shown smaller until the booking site is live, then you can promote it to primary. Minimum tap height 48px — under Google's and Apple's own touch-target guidance, anything smaller hurts mobile usability scoring.

7. Do / Don't

Do

  • Keep red exclusive to the CTA, on every asset
  • Use real job/car photos, shot in landscape for posters, square for Instagram
  • Repeat the same CTA wording site-wide ("Message us on Instagram") so it's instantly recognisable
  • Match the Meta ad's CTA button to its actual destination — if the ad opens Instagram DMs, the button must say "Send Message," not "Book Now"

Don't

  • Don't stretch or recolour the logo per-poster — one approved version, used as-is
  • Don't use brass as a text colour — it fails accessibility contrast on light backgrounds (tested 2.86:1, needs 4.5:1); hairlines and badges only
  • Don't add a second accent colour "just for this one poster" — it breaks the pattern viewers are learning
  • Don't run a Meta ad with a "Learn More" button pointing at an Instagram profile — Meta's policy requires the CTA to reflect the actual on-click action

8. Compliance checkpoints (2026)

Check Why it matters here
Contrast ratio White text on Signal Red = 5.0:1, on Navy = 15.3:1 — both pass WCAG AA. Never put white text on Brass (3.1:1, fails for normal text).
Tap targets Every button ≥48px tall on mobile — below Google's mobile-usability threshold, buttons get mis-tapped and hurt conversion.
Meta CTA-destination match Ad objective (Messages vs Traffic) must match the on-screen button copy, or the ad can get flagged in review/lower quality ranking.
Google Business Profile Keep the same phone number, service area wording and primary CTA verb across GBP, website and posters — consistency is a local-SEO trust signal.
